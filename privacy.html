Privacy Policy

AA Soft Park · Effective date: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all Android apps published by AA Soft Park ("we", "us") on Google Play, including Stack Tower and any other apps listed on our developer page (the "Apps").

1. Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number inside the Apps.

2. Third-party services

  • Google AdMob – serves ads. Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses data from partner apps
  • Google User Messaging Platform – asks for consent where required (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions).
  • Google Play Billing – processes in-app purchases.

3. Your choices

  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • If you are in a region where consent is required, you can change your consent at any time using the "Privacy settings" button in the App.
  • Purchasing "Remove Ads" removes banner and interstitial ads. Optional rewarded ads (that you choose to watch) remain available.
  • Uninstalling an App deletes all data it stored on your device.

4. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share data with third parties other than the Google services listed above, which act under their own privacy policies.

5. Data security and retention

Data stored on your device stays there until you uninstall the App or clear its data. All network communication with Google services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

6. Children

The Apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Data deletion requests

Because we do not run our own servers or store your data, there is nothing on our side to delete. To delete data held by Google (for ads), use your Google account settings or contact Google. You may still email us with any request and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when we do.
